Complete Process Development

Developing a successful UV Nanoimprint Lithography (UV-NIL) process requires much more than selecting a resist and a working stamp. Every application has unique requirements regarding resolution, substrate compatibility, throughput, defect density and production yield. NanoImprint Consulting supports customers throughout the complete development cycle—from proof of concept and feasibility studies to robust manufacturing processes. Whether developing a completely new process or improving an existing one, every project follows a structured engineering approach to maximize reproducibility, productivity and process stability.

The objective is to establish a reliable, high-yield and production-ready UV Nanoimprint Lithography process while minimizing development time and reducing manufacturing costs.

Optimization Strategy

Every parameter influences the final imprint quality. Process optimization includes pressure, contact force, UV exposure, curing conditions, demolding behavior and process repeatability. A systematic Design of Experiments (DoE) methodology can be applied to rapidly identify the optimal process window while reducing development time and experimental costs. Optimization is performed considering the complete process chain, including substrate preparation, surface treatment, resist coating, imprinting, UV curing, demolding and post-processing to ensure the highest level of process stability.
