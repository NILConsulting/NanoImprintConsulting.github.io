About NanoImprint Consulting

NanoImprint Consulting is an independent consulting company specialized in UV Nanoimprint Lithography (UV-NIL) technology solutions for semiconductor, photonics and advanced nanofabrication applications.

With more than 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry and more than 15 years dedicated to Nanoimprint Lithography, NanoImprint Consulting supports companies and research organizations in developing reliable and high-performance NIL technologies.

The expertise covers the complete UV-NIL ecosystem, including working stamp formation, backplane selection, resist formulation, anti-sticking layer engineering, process development and equipment optimization.
